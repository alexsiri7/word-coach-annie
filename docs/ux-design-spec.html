⚠️
In Throne Room (Ch. 1), Lord Ashford has "grey eyes." Here you wrote "blue eyes."

Behind him, Kael clutched the reins of his horse. The boy hadn't spoken since they left Thornwall at dawn.

⚠️
Timeline: In Departure at Dawn, the group left from Sunstone Keep, not Thornwall. Thornwall is the Ashford garrison they're trying to avoid.

The snow had started falling an hour ago, and the trail ahead was disappearing under a white blanket. They needed to reach the pass before nightfall or risk being stranded.

Key changes from current: AI-powered consistency alerts appear inline when potential contradictions are detected. Alerts show the conflicting reference, link to the source, and offer quick-fix actions. An alert counter in the toolbar lets the writer address them when ready. "Not an error" feedback trains the system.
