Chapter Twelve

The Midnight Carriage

1,240 Words Revised Dec 24

The rain against the windowpane sounded like the frantic drumming of a thousand skeletal fingers. Lord Ashbury stood before the fireplace, the dying embers casting long, dancing shadows that stretched toward Lady Eleanor as she entered the mill. The Abandoned Mill had seen better centuries, its wooden skeleton groaning under the weight of the gale.

"You shouldn't have come here, Eleanor," he said, his voice as dry as the old parchment clutched in his trembling hand. "The Discovery changes everything. If the Council finds out what we found beneath the floorboards, there will be no peace for Ashbury Manor."

Eleanor didn't flinch. She took a step forward, her silk gown rustling over the debris of forgotten grain. "The Confrontation is inevitable, Arthur. We cannot hide from the truth just because it is buried in dust."

A sudden draft extinguished the last of the fire, plunging the room into a deep, ink-stained darkness.
