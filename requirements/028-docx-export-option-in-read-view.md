## Why

Authors who want to share or further edit their manuscript in Word (or send to editors/beta readers who expect .docx) currently only have PDF and EPUB export options. A plain, consistently-formatted DOCX export closes that gap.

## What

From the Read view's download menu, a reader/author can choose "Download DOCX" alongside the existing "Download PDF" and "Download EPUB" options. The downloaded .docx file contains the manuscript (title page + part/chapter headings + scene prose, same content scope as the PDF/EPUB exports) with all body text set in Arial, 12pt, left-justified paragraphs.
