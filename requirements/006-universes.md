## Why
An author working on a series or shared world needs a container that spans multiple projects, with world objects (characters, places, elements) that appear across books.

## What
Universe entity scoping multiple projects. Universe-level characters, locations, and world elements with ordered timeline entries. Projects can be linked/unlinked to a universe.
