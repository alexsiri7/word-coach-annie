## Why
Writing assistance requires specific, curated prompts for different tasks (developmental edit vs. line edit vs. consistency check). Named prompts are more discoverable and reproducible than free-form chat.

## What
Eight writing skills registered as MCP Prompts: Developmental Edit, Line Edit, Consistency Check, Plot Structure Analysis, Character Arc Review, Scene Drafting Assistant, Outline Review, Story Development Chat. Plus four built-in coaching prompts.
