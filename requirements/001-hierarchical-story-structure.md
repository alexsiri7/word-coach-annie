## Why
Novels have inherent hierarchical organization (Parts → Chapters → Scenes) with different status levels (Outline/Draft/Revised/Final). A flat list of scenes doesn't capture this structure or support workflow-based filtering.

## What
Part → Chapter → Scene tree with per-node status tracking. API and UI for creating, updating, reordering, and deleting nodes at any level. Batch operations supported.
