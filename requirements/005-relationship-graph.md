## Why
Entities in a story are interconnected. Tracking typed relationships (APPEARS_IN, LOCATED_AT, PART_OF_PLOTLINE) allows the AI and the writer to understand the story's web.

## What
Typed relationship links between any two entities. API and UI for creating, listing, and deleting relationships.
