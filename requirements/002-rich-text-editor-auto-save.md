## Why
Writers need a polished writing experience with content versioning — not a plain textarea. Auto-save prevents data loss.

## What
Tiptap 3-based WYSIWYG editor with auto-save, word counts, and scene content versioning. Scene beats (`<!-- beat: ... -->`) rendered as styled cards inline, stripped on export.
