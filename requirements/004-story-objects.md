## Why
Complex narratives require tracking many named entities — characters, places, plotlines, world elements — and being able to reference them from scenes.

## What
CRUD for story objects of types: Character, Location, Plotline, World Element, Note. All support tags and free-form descriptions. Displayed and linked from within project views.
